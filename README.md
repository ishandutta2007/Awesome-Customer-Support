A curated list of awesome customer support software, SaaS platforms, and open-source helpdesk solutions. This repository provides a comprehensive guide for customer service teams, support agents, and businesses looking to optimize their customer experience.

## ☁️ SaaS & Hosted Platforms

Explore top commercial customer support platforms. The list is sorted by estimated company valuation.

| Product | Description | Est. Valuation / Size | Pricing (Starting Tier) | Free Tier / Trial Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **[ServiceNow CSM](https://www.servicenow.com/products/customer-service-management.html)** ⚙️ | Digital workflow platform connecting customer service with middle and back-office operations. | ~$190B (Parent) | Custom quote / Enterprise | Free Developer Instance / Sandbox access |

| **[Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/)** 🏢 | Leading omnichannel customer service and engagement platform. | ~$15B | $19 per agent/month (Support Team) | 14-day free trial (includes access to Suite Professional features) |
| **[Zoho Desk](https://www.zoho.com/desk/)** 📊 | Context-aware, multichannel helpdesk platform with built-in Zia AI assistance. | ~$12B (Parent) | $14 per user/month (Standard Plan) | Free Plan limit: up to 3 agents, email ticketing, private knowledge base, macro support |
| **[Freshdesk](https://freshdesk.com/)** 🍃 | Intuitive, cloud-based helpdesk ticketing system by Freshworks. | ~$4B | $15 per agent/month (Growth Plan) | 6-month Free Program (max 2 agents, standard email & ticketing only) / 14-day free trial |
| **[Front](https://front.com/)** ✉️ | Collaborative team inbox and powerful customer communication platform. | ~$1.7B | $25 per seat/month (Starter Plan) | 14-day free trial (access to Professional plan features, no credit card required) |
| **[Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/)** 💬 | AI-first customer service platform and advanced messenger. | ~$1.3B | $29 per seat/month (Essential Plan) | 14-day free trial (includes core plan and premium add-ons access) |
| **[LiveChat](https://www.livechat.com/)** 💬 | Real-time live chat and helpdesk platform for customer service and sales. | ~$800M | $20 per agent/month (Starter Plan) | 14-day free trial (full functionality, no credit card required) |
| **[Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/)** 🛍️ | E-commerce helpdesk designed specifically for Shopify, BigCommerce, and Magento. | ~$700M | $10/month (Starter Plan, includes 50 tickets) | 7-day free trial (all features included) |

| **[Crisp](https://crisp.chat/)** 🍋 | All-in-one multichannel messaging platform, shared inbox, CRM, and knowledge base. | ~$120M | $25/month (Pro Plan, includes 4 seats) | Free Plan limit: up to 2 seats, basic live chat widget, 30-day chat history |
| **[Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/)** 🤝 | Simple, shared inbox and highly intuitive knowledge base software. | ~$100M | $20 per user/month (Standard Plan) | Free Plan limit: up to 5 users, 1 shared inbox, 1 Docs site, 100 contacts/month (15-day free trial for paid plans) |
| **[Kayako](https://www.kayako.com/)** 🌊 | Fully integrated customer service software with live chat, email, and social tickets. | ~$60M | $15 per agent/month (Inbox Plan) | 14-day free trial (no credit card required) |

## 🌐 Open-Source GitHub Projects

Self-hosted and open-source customer support alternatives. Ordered by GitHub stars (descending).

- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/chatwoot/chatwoot?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot/stargazers) 🌟  
  Open-source customer engagement suite, an alternative to Intercom, Zendesk, and Salesforce Service Cloud.
- **[GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/glpi-project/glpi?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi/stargazers) 📋  
  Complete open-source IT asset management (ITAM) and service desk / ticketing system built in PHP.
- **[Papercups](https://github.com/papercups-io/papercups)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/papercups-io/papercups?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/papercups-io/papercups/stargazers) ☕  
  Open-source live customer chat, written in Elixir. Alternative to Intercom and Drift.
- **[Zammad](https://github.com/zammad/zammad)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/zammad/zammad?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/zammad/zammad/stargazers) 🎫  
  Web-based, open-source helpdesk/customer support system with many features to manage customer communication.
- **[FreeScout](https://github.com/freescout-helpdesk/freescout)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/freescout-helpdesk/freescout?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/freescout-helpdesk/freescout/stargazers) 🦅  
  Super lightweight free open-source help desk and shared inbox, heavily inspired by Help Scout.
- **[osTicket](https://github.com/osTicket/osTicket)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/osTicket/osTicket?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/osTicket/osTicket/stargazers) 🎟️  
  Widely-used open-source support ticket system that seamlessly routes inquiries created via email, web forms, and phone calls.
- **[Chaskiq](https://github.com/chaskiq/chaskiq)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/chaskiq/chaskiq?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/chaskiq/chaskiq/stargazers) 💬  
  Open-source conversational marketing platform and live chat software built with Ruby on Rails.
- **[Frappe Helpdesk](https://github.com/frappe/helpdesk)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/helpdesk?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/helpdesk/stargazers) 🚀  
  Modern open-source ticket management and customer support platform built on the Frappe framework and Vue.js.
- **[Peppermint](https://github.com/Peppermint-Lab/peppermint)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Peppermint-Lab/peppermint?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Peppermint-Lab/peppermint/stargazers) 🌿  
  Modern, open-source ticket management system and issue tracker built on Node.js and React.
- **[Live Helper Chat](https://github.com/LiveHelperChat/livehelperchat)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/LiveHelperChat/livehelperchat?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/LiveHelperChat/livehelperchat/stargazers) 💬  
  Feature-rich open-source live support chat for websites and mobile applications.
- **[Helpy](https://github.com/helpyio/helpy)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/helpyio/helpy?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/helpyio/helpy/stargazers) 🆘  
  Open-source customer helpdesk in Ruby on Rails. Features a multichannel ticketing system and knowledgebase.
- **[Trudesk](https://github.com/polonel/trudesk)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/polonel/trudesk?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/polonel/trudesk/stargazers) 🛠️  
  Open-source helpdesk/ticketing solution built on Node.js and MongoDB.
- **[ITFlow](https://github.com/itflow-org/itflow)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/itflow-org/itflow?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/itflow-org/itflow/stargazers) ⚡  
  Open-source IT documentation, ticketing, client management, and invoicing platform for MSPs and internal IT teams.
- **[UVdesk](https://github.com/uvdesk/community-skeleton)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/uvdesk/community-skeleton?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/uvdesk/community-skeleton/stargazers) 📝  
  Enterprise-grade, open-source helpdesk system built on Symfony for standard and e-commerce support.
- **[OpenSupports](https://github.com/opensupports/opensupports)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/opensupports/opensupports?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/opensupports/opensupports/stargazers) 🎫  
  Open-source ticket system and knowledge base built with PHP and React.
- **[Request Tracker (RT)](https://github.com/bestpractical/rt)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bestpractical/rt?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/bestpractical/rt/stargazers) 🛡️  
  Battle-tested, enterprise-grade issue tracking and customer ticketing system used by organizations worldwide.
- **[Znuny](https://github.com/znuny/Znuny)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/znuny/Znuny?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/znuny/Znuny/stargazers) 🦓  
  The community-driven, open-source ticket request system and fork of OTRS.
